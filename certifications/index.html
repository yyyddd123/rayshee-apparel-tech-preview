Quick Answers — Frequently Asked Questions

What is your MOQ?

50 pieces per style-colorway after sample approval. Mixed sizes are allowed within one color.

How long for sample production & delivery?

Sample production: 7–10 working days. Shipped via DHL / UPS / FedEx / TNT / Aramex, transit normally 3–7 business days (subject to destination & customs clearance). Shipping cost to be confirmed after receiving your delivery address.

What payment options do you accept?

Samples: PayPal / Credit Card / T/T via cart checkout. Bulk orders: T/T, Xtransfer, WorldFirst, Mybank Foreign-Trade E-Collect; support both inquiry-form and cart checkout.

Can I order samples before bulk order?

Yes. We highly recommend sample validation. Add-to-cart and check-out directly on product pages.
