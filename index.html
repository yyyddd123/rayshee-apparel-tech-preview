03 · Universal-Fit

One Pattern. Lower SKU Burden. Fewer Returns.

Managing sizes for mixed-gender teams and unisex collections creates heavy SKU workload and higher return rates. Our Universal-Fit unisex pattern adapts to diverse body shapes.

Business benefit for your brand

  • Cut down your total SKU count
  • Simplify inventory planning
  • Reduce fit-related customer returns
04 · Shared-Origin

Consistent fabric & quality across your whole product line.

Launch cohesive family-style outdoor collections including adults, kids, couples and even pet apparel. Every piece shares identical fabric performance, color fastness and workmanship standard.

Business benefit for your brand

  • Build unique cross-sell opportunities
  • Stand out from generic outdoor competitors
  • Keep consistent brand quality across your full collection

Quick Answers — Frequently Asked Questions

What is your MOQ?

50 pieces per style-colorway after sample approval. Mixed sizes are allowed within one color.

How long for sample production & delivery?

Sample production: 7–10 working days. Shipped via DHL / UPS / FedEx / TNT / Aramex, transit normally 3–7 business days (subject to destination & customs clearance). Shipping cost to be confirmed after receiving your delivery address.

What payment options do you accept?

Samples: PayPal / Credit Card / T/T via cart checkout. Bulk orders: T/T, Xtransfer, WorldFirst, Mybank Foreign-Trade E-Collect; support both inquiry-form and cart checkout.

Can I order samples before bulk order?

Yes. We highly recommend sample validation. Add-to-cart and check-out directly on product pages.
