Detailed Product Specification

  1. Made of professional functional weft-knit fabric, including kinetic quick-dry pique knit for golf and pickleball polo, stretch tech-knit, sunproof and windproof composite tech-knit softshell. Custom woven shell fabrics are also available.
  2. Borderless ergonomic cutting with dynamic Universal Fit, simplifying large-group procurement sizing and lowering SKU & return risks.
  3. Performance Specs: High breathability, advanced moisture-wicking, abrasion-resistant knit structure built for high-intensity outdoor sports.
  4. Blank plain design perfect for sports team uniforms, event outfits, camp groups, dance studios and gorpcore brand Private Label.
  5. Suitable for golf, tennis, pickleball, motorsport racing, yachting, dance competitions, camp study tours, charity trail events, frisbee, camping and hiking.
  6. Full custom logo, Private Label, and custom brand hangtags support (Full Pantone corporate color accuracy).
  7. Flexible production scaling system, available for initial wholesale evaluation orders (50pcs per style-colorway post sample approval).
  8. As a leading borderless-casual light outdoor tech-apparel factory, we provide full ODM/OEM services for sports clubs, event organizers and outdoor brands worldwide.
  9. Custom-engineered technical woven waterproof garments for moderate-to-harsh outdoor environments can be manufactured on OEM request.
  10. Accessory Bundled Service: We provide matching sports towels and customized team caps for all team-uniform & sports-apparel orders, helping clients complete unified brand-image building for sports events and club activities.
